# Issued P&ID set

Engineering drawings, one or two sheets per process unit, generated by
`python tools/make_pids.py --png` from `tools/pid_kit.py` and the symbol
library in `docs/symbols/`. They are drawn to ISA-5.1 conventions: bubble
shape carries the mounting location, line style carries the service, and
every control valve shows its fail position.

## Why the drawings connect where they do

`pid_kit` rasterises each symbol and measures the ink rather than trusting
its bounding box. `Placed.left` / `.right` sit on the connection axis, so a
control valve lands on the pipe instead of hanging below it, and
`Placed.left_at(y)` / `.right_at(y)` follow a shaped symbol such as the
fired heater, which is narrower at its convection section than at its
firebox. Route pipes to those ports and the drawing stays correct if a
symbol is ever swapped for a different one.

## Reviewing a change

Render the sheet and look at the PNG. Reading coordinates does not catch
overlapping labels, leaders that end in mid air, or a line that stops a few
pixels short of a nozzle.
